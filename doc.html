1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the Estate Pilot Real Estate Platform, a comprehensive web application designed for managing residential and commercial plot sales. This platform provides a seamless experience for customers to browse and book plots, while offering powerful management tools for administrators and agents.

1.1 Key Features

  • Plot Management: Browse, search, and book residential/commercial plots
  • Flexible Payment Plans: Support for booking payments and installment plans (Monthly/Quarterly)
  • Multi-Role System: Separate portals for Customers, Agents, and Administrators
  • SMS Notifications: Automated notifications for bookings, payments, and reminders
  • Payment Tracking: Complete payment history and invoice generation
  • Agent Commission: Track and manage agent commissions and payments

1.2 Who Should Use This Manual?

  • Customers: Learn how to browse plots, make bookings, and manage payments
  • Agents: Understand how to track referrals and commission payments
  • Administrators: Master the complete system management and configuration

2.2 Registration Process

New users must register an account to book plots and access the customer portal.

How to Register a New Account
  1. Go to https://londonabd.com/register or click the "Register" button on the top-right of the homepage.
  2. Fill in the registration form:
    • Full Name — Enter your complete name as it will appear on bookings.
    • Email Address — Provide a valid email (used for notifications).
    • Phone Number — Enter your mobile number (this will be verified via OTP).
    • Address — Your residential or business address.
    • Password — Create a strong password (min. 8 characters).
  3. Click the "Register" button to submit the form.
  4. You will be redirected to the Phone Verification page. A 6-digit OTP code is sent to your phone via SMS.
  5. Enter the OTP code within 10 minutes. If the code expires, click "Resend OTP" to receive a new one.
  6. Once verified, your account is activated. You will be redirected to the login page.
Tip: Use a phone number you have active access to — the OTP verification is mandatory and cannot be skipped.

2.3 Login Process

User Type Login URL Description
Customer https://londonabd.com/login For regular users and plot buyers
Agent https://londonabd.com/login For sales agents and brokers

2.4 Forgot Password

If you forget your password, you can reset it via SMS verification:

How to Reset Your Password
  1. On the login page, click the "Forgot Password" link below the password field.
  2. Enter your registered phone number in the field provided.
  3. Click "Send OTP" — a 6-digit verification code will be sent to your phone via SMS.
  4. Enter the OTP code on the verification screen.
  5. Once verified, you will be prompted to create a new password.
  6. Enter your new password, confirm it, and click "Reset Password".
  7. You'll be redirected to the login page. Log in with your new password.

7.2 Plot Management

URL: https://londonabd.com/admin/plots

Complete control over all plot listings in the system.

How to Add a New Plot
  1. Navigate to Admin Sidebar → Plots → All Plots.
  2. Click the "+ Add New Plot" button at the top right.
  3. Fill in the Basic Information:
    • Title / Name, Plot Number, Size (Katha), Block, Road Number, Road Width, Facing Direction, Plot Category
  4. Fill in Pricing & Payment:
    • Total Price — Full price of the plot.
    • Booking Payment — Amount required to reserve (remaining is auto-calculated).
    • Installment Plan — Select Monthly or Quarterly.
    • Total Installments — Number of installments.
    • Per Installment Amount — Amount per installment.
  5. Add Additional Details: Description, Plot Map/Image (JPEG, PNG, or PDF up to 5MB), Google Maps Location, Nearby Facilities.
  6. Set Status to "Available" to list it publicly.
  7. Click "Save Plot" to create the listing.
How to Edit an Existing Plot
  1. Go to Admin → Plots and find the plot in the list.
  2. Click the "Edit" button (pencil icon) on the plot row.
  3. Modify any fields as needed and click "Update Plot".
Important: Only plots with "Available" status can be edited. If a plot is Booked or Sold, you must cancel/remove all associated bookings first before editing.
How to Search and Filter Plots
  1. Go to Admin → Plots.
  2. Use the Search field to search by plot title, plot number, block, or road number.
  3. Use the Status dropdown to filter by: Available, Pending, Booked, or Sold.
  4. Adjust Per Page to show 10, 25, 50, or 100 results.
  5. Click "Filter" to apply. Click "Clear" to reset all filters.

Plot Management Quick Reference:

  • View: Click eye icon to see complete plot details and booking history
  • Edit: Click pencil icon to update plot information (available plots only)
  • Delete: Click trash icon to remove a plot listing (available plots only)

7.3 Booking Management

URL: https://londonabd.com/admin/bookings

Manage all customer bookings and approvals.

How to View and Filter Bookings
  1. Navigate to Admin Sidebar → Bookings.
  2. Use the Search field to find bookings by customer name, phone, email, or plot number.
  3. Filter by Status: Pending, Approved, Pre-Booked, or Cancelled.
  4. Filter by Date Range (From / To) to narrow results.
  5. Click "Filter" to apply. Click "Clear" to reset.
  6. Click "Export CSV" to download filtered results as a spreadsheet.
How to Approve or Update a Booking
  1. Go to Admin → Bookings and click "View" (eye icon) on a booking.
  2. Review booking details: customer info, plot details, referral agent, and payment plan.
  3. To change the booking status, use the "Status" dropdown at the top:
    • Pending → Initial state when customer submits booking.
    • Pre-Booked → Temporary reservation with expiry date.
    • Approved → Confirms the booking (plot status changes to "Booked" automatically).
    • Cancelled → Cancels the booking (plot becomes "Available" again).
  4. Optionally add Additional Info / Notes in the text area.
  5. Click "Update Booking" to save changes.
  6. The customer receives an SMS notification about the status change.
How to Create a Manual Booking (Walk-in / Offline Customer)
  1. Go to Admin → Bookings and click the "+ Create Booking" button.
  2. Select a Customer from the dropdown (only active users are listed).
  3. Select an Available Plot from the plot dropdown.
  4. The form auto-fills the plot's pricing and installment plan details.
  5. Optionally assign a Referral Agent from the agent dropdown.
  6. Click "Create Booking" to submit.
  7. The booking is created and the customer is notified via SMS.
How to Change the Referral Agent on a Booking
  1. Open the booking detail page by clicking "View" on a booking.
  2. In the Agent section, use the "Change Agent" dropdown to select a different agent.
  3. Click "Update Agent" to save the change.

Booking Status Flow:

Pending → Pre-Booked → Approved → Completed (or Cancelled at any stage)

7.4 Payment Management

URL: https://londonabd.com/admin/payments

Record and track all customer payments.

How to Record a New Payment
  1. Navigate to Admin Sidebar → Payments.
  2. Click the "+ Record Payment" button.
  3. In the Booking field, type the customer name, phone number, or plot number to search. Select the correct booking from the dropdown.
  4. The system displays the booking's financial summary: total price, amount paid, outstanding balance, installment details, and partial payment progress.
  5. Select the Payment Type:
    • Booking Payment — Initial payment to secure the reservation.
    • Installment Payment — Full installment amount (marks one installment as paid).
    • Part Of Installment — Partial installment payment (accumulates toward a full installment).
    • Full Payment — Pays the entire remaining balance at once.
  6. Enter the Amount (auto-suggested based on payment type).
  7. Add an optional Payment Note (e.g., bank reference, cash receipt number).
  8. Click "Save Payment" to record it.
  9. The customer automatically receives an SMS notification confirming the payment.
How to Generate and Download an Invoice
  1. Go to Admin → Payments.
  2. Find the payment in the list (use search/filter if needed).
  3. Click the "Invoice" button (printer icon) on the payment row.
  4. The invoice page opens with a professional PDF-ready layout showing all payment and booking details.
  5. Click "Print" to print or save as PDF.
How to Search and Export Payments
  1. Go to Admin → Payments.
  2. Use the Search field to find payments by customer name, phone, or plot number.
  3. Filter by Booking ID to see all payments for a specific booking.
  4. Filter by Date Range to narrow results by time period.
  5. Click "Export CSV" to download the filtered payment data as a spreadsheet.
Caution: Deleting a payment is permanent and cannot be undone. Only delete payments that were recorded in error. The customer will receive an SMS notification about the deletion.

Payment Types Reference:

Type Description When to Use
Booking Payment Initial payment to secure the plot First payment when a new booking is approved
Installment Payment Full scheduled installment Customer pays the full monthly/quarterly installment amount
Part Of Installment Partial amount toward an installment Customer pays less than the full installment amount
Full Payment Complete remaining balance Customer wants to clear the entire outstanding amount at once

8. PAYMENT & INSTALLMENT SYSTEM

The platform supports flexible payment options including booking payments and structured installment plans (Monthly/Quarterly).

8.1 Payment Structure

Stage Description Typical Amount
1. Booking Payment Initial payment to secure plot reservation 10-30% of Total Price
2. Installments Regular scheduled payments over agreed period Monthly / Quarterly
3. Final Payment Complete remaining balance to finalize ownership Outstanding Balance

8.2 Installment Plans

Monthly Plan:

Pay in equal monthly installments over the agreed period.

  • Fixed monthly amount
  • Flexible tenure (12-60 months)
  • Auto-calculated payments
  • SMS payment reminders

Quarterly Plan:

Pay every three months with larger installment amounts.

  • Quarterly payment schedule
  • Lower number of transactions
  • Larger per-payment amount
  • Due date notifications

8.3 Payment Tracking & Progress

What Customers Can Track:

  • Total Paid Amount: Sum of all payments made to date
  • Outstanding Balance: Remaining amount to be paid
  • Paid Installments: Number of installments completed

  • Next Due Date: Date of next installment payment
  • Payment Progress: Percentage of total amount paid

9. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ

9.1 Login & Access Issues

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered phone number, verify the OTP sent via SMS, and create a new password.

Q: I'm not receiving the OTP code.

A: Check the following:

  • Verify your phone number is correct
  • Check your phone's SMS inbox and spam folder
  • Ensure you have mobile network coverage
  • Click "Resend OTP" to request a new code
  • Contact support if the issue persists

Q: My account is locked/disabled.

A: Contact the administrator to reactivate your account. Your account may have been temporarily disabled for security or administrative reasons.

9.2 Booking & Plot Issues

Q: The plot I want to book shows as unavailable.

A: The plot may be already booked by another customer or temporarily reserved. Contact the sales team for alternative options or to check if the plot will become available.

Q: How long does it take for my booking to be confirmed?

A: Bookings are typically confirmed within 24-48 hours after submission. You'll receive an SMS notification once your booking is approved by the admin.

Q: Can I cancel or change my booking?

A: Contact the administrator to request booking modifications or cancellations. Cancellation policies and refund terms will be communicated by the admin team.

9.3 Payment Issues

Q: I made a payment but it's not showing in my account.

A: Payment updates may take a few hours to reflect. The admin team records payments manually after receiving them. If it's been more than 24 hours, contact the admin with your payment proof.

Q: How can I download my payment invoice?

A: Go to My Account → Payments, find the payment record, and click the "Download Invoice" button. The invoice will be generated as a PDF.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?

A: Contact the sales team for available payment methods. Typically, payments can be made via bank transfer, cash payment at office, or mobile banking.

Q: When is my next installment due?

A: Check your customer dashboard for the "Next Due Date" information. You'll also receive SMS reminders before the due date.

9.4 Technical Issues

Q: The website is not loading properly.

A: Try the following steps:

  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Check your internet connection
  • Disable browser extensions temporarily
  • Update your browser to the latest version

Q: Images are not displaying correctly.

A: This may be due to slow internet connection or browser cache issues. Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache.

Q: The website is slow to respond.

A: Check your internet speed. If the problem persists across different networks, it may be a server issue. Contact technical support.
